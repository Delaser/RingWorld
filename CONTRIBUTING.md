# Contributing to RingWorld

RingWorld is under private development and is not currently accepting
unsolicited source-code, asset, or documentation contributions.

Bug reports, reproduction steps, compatibility observations, performance
measurements, and gameplay feedback are welcome through a project channel made
available by the owner. Do not include third-party or decompiled Minecraft
code in a report.

## Future contribution path

The project intends to support invited contributions later. Before any
copyrightable work is accepted:

1. the project owner must approve the scope;
2. the contributor must confirm they have the right to submit the work;
3. the contributor must accept the then-current RingWorld contributor
   agreement; and
4. the change must satisfy RingWorld's documentation, testing, loader-support,
   and compatibility requirements.

The contributor agreement will preserve the owner's ability to distribute,
protect, and relicense RingWorld while recording contributor attribution. No
licence or ownership transfer is implied by opening an issue, discussing an
idea, or sending an unsolicited patch.

## Compatibility forks and ports

A narrowly scoped compatibility patch, modpack adaptation, loader port, or
fork may be approved by prior written permission. Permission is not automatic.
Requests should explain:

- the exact modpack, loader, Minecraft version, or incompatibility involved;
- why the change cannot reasonably live in upstream RingWorld;
- who will maintain and distribute it;
- how users will obtain matching source or support information, if required;
- confirmation that it will remain noncommercial; and
- how RingWorld ownership, attribution, branding, and security updates will be
  preserved.

The terms in the written approval control that work. Unless approval explicitly
says otherwise, the RingWorld Evaluation License prohibits modification,
forking, and redistribution.
