## RingWorld 1.2 — Minecraft 26.1.x ({{LOADER}})

- Added Low, Medium and High distant-ring detail settings, switchable with `/ringworld lod`. Each player can choose their own level.
- Added distant torch and village lighting, and rock colours on steep Atlas slopes.
- Improved the blend between real chunks and the distant ring, including height transitions and weather shading.
- Moved Atlas mesh preparation off the render thread and fixed updates being discarded during large downloads.
- Added wall materials, decay and pattern previews. New worlds default to RingWorld materials with the RingWorld Structure pattern; Masonry and Gradient are also available.
- Added machinery, channels and other structural details to generated walls. Distant walls use the selected pattern, and newly generated bottommost bedrock uses the wall materials.
- Fixed terrain disappearing and lighting turning dark when the camera moves outside the ring walls.
- Added seed previews and separate sky and sun controls.
- Raised the Atlas cell limit to fit the Large preset and added guidance for larger custom rings.
- Fixed a Lithium point-of-interest mixin conflict on both loaders.
