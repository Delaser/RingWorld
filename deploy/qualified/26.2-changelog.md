## RingWorld 1.1 — Minecraft 26.2 ({{LOADER}})

RingWorld for Minecraft 26.2. This is a separate build from the 26.1–26.1.2
files; use the jar matching your Minecraft version and mod loader.

- A finite Overworld with continuous travel and interaction through the seam.
- Curved nearby terrain and a progressively generated full-ring Atlas.
- Small, Medium and Large presets, plus custom ring dimensions.
- Version-specific rendering, menu and world-generation adapters for 26.2.

{{INSTALL_NOTE}}

The distant ring takes time to generate. Real chunks are playable first;
a fogged placeholder fills in with terrain colours, followed by detailed
height geometry once generation is complete. Progress is saved and resumes
after restarting. Larger rings take longer, and hardware, seed and server
load all affect the time required.

Back up worlds before upgrading. Use a copy when trying a newer Minecraft
version; do not open an upgraded save in an older version. The previous
26.1.2 release is a rollback option only with its matching pre-upgrade backup.

Third-party renderer/mod compatibility and Minecraft's experimental Vulkan
backend are not covered by the OpenGL qualification results.
